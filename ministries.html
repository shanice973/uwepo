The Bigger Picture

Parousia Global

Uwepo Church is the first expression of Parousia Global — a movement of people sent to carry God's presence and His manifestations all over the earth, in every sphere of human interaction.

We are not a denomination, but a family of believers with one defining theme: A People of the Presence.

Learn More About Us

Members are characterized by:

  • Passion and love for God and fellow men
  • Scriptural in-depth comprehension — believers full of the Word
  • A lifestyle of prayer — revival-conscious believers
  • Carriers of the presence and the supernatural
  • Eternity consciousness and preparation
  • All-round excellence and top-notch good character
  • Responsible and destiny-oriented ambassadors of Christ
  • Useful in family, the Body of Christ and nation-building
